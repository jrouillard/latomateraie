Architecture

The cottage is a beautifully renovated former wine cellar, built from traditional local stone. Exposed wooden beams and locally made terracotta floor tiles add to its authentic charm.

On the ground floor, you will find a spacious 28 m² living room with a fireplace, and a 10 m² kitchen opening onto the living area. There is also a bathroom with a bathtub and toilet.

Outside, a south-west-facing terrace offers the perfect spot to relax, while a small, flower-filled garden with a securely covered well enjoys a sunny, south-facing aspect.

Layout and amenities

A fully equipped kitchen and all the essentials for a comfortable stay (washing machine, dryer, rice cooker).

The living room features a fireplace, sofa, armchairs, extendable dining table, TV, DVD player, hi-fi system, internet access, books and DVDs.

Large French doors open onto the sunny terrace, creating a bright and welcoming living space.

activities

Abundant wildlife

Just 80 metres from the cottage, the marshland is home to a wide variety of resident and migratory birds, including kingfishers, storks, stilts, grey and white herons, egrets, swans, wild ducks, geese, harriers and many more.

Biking and walking

Just 80 metres from the cottage, a biking path invites you to explore the peaceful, wildlife-rich marshland on foot or by bike. Following cycle paths and quiet country roads, you can reach the Wild Coast (18 km), passing through Marennes (8 km), La Tremblade (9 km) or Ronce-les-Bains (12 km), with its beautiful sandy beaches.
